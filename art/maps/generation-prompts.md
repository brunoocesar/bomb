Ferramenta: geração de imagens integrada (`image_gen`), três chamadas independentes. Os PNGs selecionados foram copiados para `art/maps/tutorial/`, preservando os originais e a transparência do atlas.

## objects-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite atlas for Bomb Your Way!, a cute 2D top-down grid adventure in Roblox ScreenGui. Create ONE transparent PNG atlas, square, EXACTLY 4 columns by 5 rows, 20 equally sized square cells. Every sprite centered in its own cell with 10 percent transparent padding. Read strictly row major left to right. Row 1: (1) permanent rounded grey mossy stone block seen slightly from above, (2) destructible warm wooden crate with diagonal slat, (3) wooden energy crate with large glowing cyan four-point diamond crystal in center, (4) wooden bonus crate with black bomb silhouette badge. Row 2: (1) wooden range bonus crate with simple orange radiating rays badge, (2) secret weathered wooden crate with clear diagonal crack and tiny violet glint, (3) treasure wooden crate with gold coin badge, (4) closed exit gate: rounded pale stone arch with two turquoise inset lamps, closed dark teal wooden door and large readable padlock. Row 3: (1) OPEN version of exactly that same stone gate, wooden doors swung apart, bright turquoise portal opening and simple pale upward arrow, (2) bomb capacity pickup: black round bomb with tiny gold plus badge, (3) range pickup: glowing orange diamond with four chunky outward rays, (4) three stacked gold coins. Row 4: (1) rescue pickup: cute turquoise frog peeking out of a cream spotted egg, (2) secret reward: purple and gold star medallion, (3) placed black round bomb with short fuse tiny orange spark, (4) enemy: cute purple rounded grumpy slime with cream eyes. Row 5: (1) orange/yellow explosion burst core, (2) horizontal straight orange/yellow flame segment, (3) vertical straight orange/yellow flame segment, (4) warm yellow four-point energy sparkle. Style: polished SIMPLE cartoon 2D raster game art, chunky dark navy outlines, rounded silhouettes, vibrant controlled colors, minimal details, soft flat two-tone shading, consistent top-down three-quarter sprite projection, no photorealism, no pixel art, no text, no captions, no labels, no grid lines, no borders between cells, no UI, no ground plane. Preserve genuine transparent alpha, no checkerboard painted into background. Gates must be recognizable and consistent in closed/open state. No sprite may cross cell boundaries.

## garden-ground-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape background image for the playable Garden Gate map in Bomb Your Way!, a simple cute 2D cartoon top-down grid adventure. Create one landscape illustration with a 13:9 aspect ratio. STRICT overhead view of a quiet grassy garden clearing. Muted sage green and soft mint grass with a few tiny rounded grass tufts and subtle broad soft patches. All the central 85 percent of the picture must remain evenly colored calm walkable ground, very low contrast, unobstructed and suitable behind modular sprites. Decorative small rounded bushes, two tiny cream flowers, moss and warm light only at the extreme corners and outermost edges; keep them sparse. Style: simple hand-drawn cartoon game background, rounded forms, soft two-tone shading, very reduced details, less saturated than orange protagonist and cyan energy crates. No characters, no enemies, no bombs, no crates, no walls, no doors, no fences, no paths dividing the play area, no perspective horizon, no grid, no text, no logos. Fill the entire canvas. This is the ground layer only; all collision objects and exit are separate sprites placed later. A charming readable garden rather than a dramatic scenic vista.

## courtyard-ground-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape background image for the playable Rescue Courtyard map in Bomb Your Way!, a simple cute 2D cartoon top-down grid adventure. Create one landscape illustration with a 13:9 aspect ratio. STRICT overhead view of a quiet sunny garden courtyard ground, soft warm pale sandstone mixed with muted sage moss. A broad uninterrupted creamy beige flat courtyard area fills the central 85 percent. Very subtle irregular large paving seams and small soft moss spots, low contrast and sparse. Tiny rounded leaves and a couple of simple cream flowers only at the extreme corners and outermost edge, no raised objects in the playable center. Simple polished cartoon 2D game art, soft two-tone shading, rounded shapes, minimal details, restrained low saturation background so colorful interactive sprites remain visible. No characters, no enemies, no frog, no bombs, no crates, no walls, no doors, no fences, no grid, no horizon, no perspective scene, no text or logos. Ground layer only, collision objects and gate placed separately afterward. Fill canvas entirely; calm readable overhead courtyard.

O resultado do atlas tem 1254×1254 pixels e espaçamento não perfeitamente uniforme. A integração usa os limites medidos por sprite, não a grade ideal descrita no prompt.

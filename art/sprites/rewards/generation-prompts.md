# Packs dos segredos da fase 1

Gerados com a skill imagegen por edição dos atlas originais. Os originais gerados são preservados; cópias para importação são reduzidas uniformemente a 1024×1024 pelo script Prepare-RewardPackImports.ps1. O sapinho usa a arte base em ambos os packs.

## cream / hero

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. This image is the EDIT TARGET, a complete production sprite sheet for Bomb Your Way!, NOT a style reference. Make a cosmetic CREAM BODY variant by changing ONLY the orange protagonist's orange body, arms and feet to pale warm cream with soft golden cream shadows. Preserve its slightly lighter cream belly distinction. Keep the fuse and flame orange/yellow, eyes black, expression identical, dark outlines unchanged. CRITICAL INVARIANTS: preserve every sprite's EXACT position, scale, silhouette, pose, framing, transparent gutters and ground support point. Preserve the exact source canvas size 1254x1254 and exact existing 8 columns x 7 rows, FIFTY-SIX frames. Do not add or remove frames, do not shuffle, do not redraw the character or change facial expressions. Do not crop the sheet. Preserve genuine alpha transparency. No text, no labels, no new accessories, no painted checkerboard, no shadows outside original silhouette. This is the full pack's hero atlas; matching idle/walk/mounted variants will be integrated together, with the unchanged original frog atlas.
```

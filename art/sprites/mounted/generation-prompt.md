NEW asset: protagonist RIDING the frog as ONE unified character sprite; seated naturally astride frog's upper back, knees bent and feet visible beside frog, never standing on frog's face. Frog must remain instantly recognizable and clearly visible below rider: entire face and eyes visible in front view; head, eye, chin and front/rear legs visible in side views; rear haunches and sides clearly visible in rear view. Rider smaller than frog head width. No reins, saddles, clothes or new accessories. Orthographic game perspective matching references. EXACT 4 columns x 4 rows, sixteen separate evenly spaced sprites in equal square cells, generous transparent gutters with nothing crossing cell edges. Row 1: facing DOWN toward viewer, four subtle seated walking poses frog legs alternating. Row 2: facing LEFT, four subtle walking poses. Row 3: facing RIGHT, four subtle walking poses. Row 4: facing UP away from viewer, four subtle walking poses. Same size and same ground-contact baseline in ALL sixteen cells, rider fuse at same height, frog feet on same line, no jumping displacement. Center whole mounted pair horizontally in each cell. Use frog face as focus in front row; rear row BOTH rider and frog face away, no front-facing eyes. Entire body intact in every cell. Transparent alpha background, NO text, NO labels, NO borders, NO drop shadows, NO checkerboard painted into image. The sheet will be sliced on an exact uniform 4x4 grid.

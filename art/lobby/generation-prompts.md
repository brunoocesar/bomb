The attached images are style references ONLY; don't include any sprites, character, frog, UI, text or atlas grid. Create a wide 16:9 landscape illustration of a cozy little adventure camp in a forest clearing. Match the references' rounded cartoon shapes, dark navy-brown outlines, warm hand-painted cel shading and simple charming details. Muted sage/teal trees frame the distant background, blue-green wooded hills, soft golden sunlight through canopy. Two small cream-and-orange camping tents at the far left and far back right, a few wooden supply crates with metal corner brackets, mossy rounded stones, hanging warm lanterns, a tiny soft campfire with gentle smoke in the far-left background, a winding sandy path leaving camp toward the right distance. MOST IMPORTANT COMPOSITION: foreground center from x=35% to65%, y=45% to85% is a clear, flat, warmly lit sandy circular clearing, EMPTY with no props, ready for the game's separate large character sprites. Leftmost 22% and rightmost 27% have low contrast scenery because navigation and quest cards will be overlaid there. Keep the center stage uncluttered, horizon high near top third. Decorative shrubs mostly at the outer edges. Broad soft shading, subdued backdrop saturation so orange hero and turquoise frog stand out. Fits existing cartoon 2D sprite style, NOT realistic, NOT 3D render, NOT pixel art, NOT isometric, no people, no animals, no labels, no logos, no words, no UI, no drawn character shadows in central empty clearing. Fully opaque background image. Compose to also crop gracefully to portrait with center clearing intact.
